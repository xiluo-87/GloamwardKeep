About this proof

The map is a review fixture on generated terrain. It uses roomier tiles with resized buildings and vegetation, while mobs keep their earlier proportions. It shows legal archer placements and moving Goblins; it is not a combat simulation.

Map inspection: scroll to zoom around the pointer, then drag to pan while zoomed. Fit map recenters the full board. Camera direction and tilt are available in the toolbar.

The custom Goblin, terrain, trees, rocks, and tower pieces are provisional art candidates. Earlier pack models remain available in the archive.
